On New Years' Eve this year a friend asked me a seemingly innocuous question: "Do you think you're a good person?"

At the time the best answer I could give was "I _try_ to be a good person, I strive towards it in my daily actions as best I can---but am I _fundamentally_ a good person? No. To say that I am implies a finality, a destination reached that would blind me to the many faults and unexamined biases I still contain."
